# Move your old AI's memory into Menerio (optional route one)

The fast route. Ten minutes, three steps, no typing.

1. In Menerio: **Settings** → **Import** → **AI Memory** tab. Click
   **Copy** on the Step 1 prompt. (It asks your AI to write out
   everything it knows about you as standalone statements, sorted into
   People, Projects, Preferences, Decisions, Professional context,
   Personal context.)
2. Paste the prompt into ChatGPT (or Claude) and send. Read the answer
   before you move it: this is everything the app's memory holds about
   you, and not all of it deserves to survive the move. Delete the lines
   that are stale or wrong. The move is also a cleaning.
3. Paste what is left into the Step 2 box. Menerio counts the items it
   detects, live. Click **Process & Import**; each statement becomes a
   note of its own.

Honest notes:

- The AI can only dump what its memory holds. It cannot read your whole
  chat history across conversations, and it will tell you so.
- Importing uses AI credits (each note gets processed and indexed).
- Statements land as notes, and people named in them get person records
  created and linked (verified 2026-08-13: a two-statement import
  reported "Created 2 people, linked 1 existing person to notes").
  Facts the machine derives from your notes wait in the Review Queue
  for your Keep.
