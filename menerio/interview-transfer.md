# The interview transfer (Chapter 27, route two)

The deep route. The dump (route one) gets what the app happened to
remember. The interview gets what actually matters, because you answer as
the source. Run it in the AI app you have been using; it knows enough
about you to ask sharp questions.

Paste this:

```
I am moving my personal context out of this app and into a permanent
memory system. Interview me to build it. Ask one question at a time,
eight questions maximum, covering: who I am and what my work really is;
the people who show up in my decisions and what matters about each; my
current projects with their real deadlines and states; my priorities in
order; how I like to be helped; and my hard limits. Use what you already
know about me to ask specific questions, not generic ones, and challenge
me where your memory and my answers disagree. At the end, output
everything as standalone statements sorted into: People, Projects,
Preferences, Decisions, Professional context, Personal context. No
flattery, no filler.
```

Answer honestly; the questions only work if you do. Then take the final
statement list to Menerio → **Settings** → **Import** → **AI Memory**,
Step 2 box, and import it (`menerio/ai-memory-transport.md`, step 3).

Do the dump first, the interview second: the interview fills the holes
the dump exposed, and the statements it produces are yours, not the
app's guesses.
