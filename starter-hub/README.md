# Starter hub

This folder becomes your file-based personal AI system (Part VI of the
book, starting at Chapter 26). Copy the whole `starter-hub` folder
somewhere permanent (Documents is fine), rename it if you like (`my-hub`
is a good name), and fill the files as Chapter 26 walks you through it.

What is here:

- `AGENTS.md` — your AI's operating manual: how to work in this folder,
  plus your red lines. Agent tools read this file automatically.
- `context/` — who you are, your people, your projects, your voice
  (the Part II pages, as files you own).
- `skills/` — one file per skill; the five starters are pre-loaded.
- `procedures.md` — the register: everything that runs on its own.
- `decisions.md` — append-only log of real decisions.
- `inbox/` — where loose captures land between weekly reviews.

Nothing here needs a terminal. It is a folder of text files, and that is
the point.
