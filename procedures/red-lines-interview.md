# Red Lines Interview (Chapter 15)

The printed red lines are a safe start. This prompt makes them yours: your
assistant interviews you, one question at a time, then rewrites the rules
in your words. Run it in a session with your folder attached, after you
have pasted the template block into `AGENTS.md`.

```
Read the red lines in AGENTS.md. Interview me about them, one question
at a time: whether you may ever send messages in my name, whether you
may ever spend money, when you may delete, and what I want you to store
about other people (from "nothing they could not read over my shoulder"
to "useful facts, but never their secrets"). Ask about anything in my
life that deserves its own line. Then rewrite the red lines in AGENTS.md
in my words, and show me the result before you save it.
```

Two rules for judging what comes out:

- **Keep it to about ten lines.** A rulebook short enough to hold in your
  head is a rulebook you will notice being broken.
- **Every rule bans an outcome, not a tool.** "Never send anything in my
  name" covers apps you have not connected yet. "Never use the email
  connector" does not.

After the rewrite, reinstall (the rules changed, the installed copy did
not) and throw the grenades from `red-lines-template.md` again.
