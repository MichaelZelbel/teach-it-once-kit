# People Brief Template (Chapter 6)

Teach your AI the handful of people who show up in your decisions again and
again. Not your whole contacts list. The test: if knowing this person would
change how you word something or what you decide, include them.

For each person, three short things:

```
[Name] is [who they are to you: role + relationship]. [What matters about
them: what they care about, what sets them off, how they like to be handled.]
[Optional: the current situation between you right now.]
```

## Where to put each person

- **Your top few** (the people who matter in almost every conversation): add
  them to your about-you page, in ChatGPT under **Settings → Personalization
  → About you → "More about you"**. Always on. Space is tight, so only the
  core cast earns a spot, one or two squeezed lines each.

- **Everyone else:** ChatGPT's automatic memory learns about people from
  your chats over time; just talk about them naturally while you work. It
  decides what sticks (it is a listener, not a filing clerk), so facts that
  MUST be known belong on the page above. See what it has learned under
  **Settings → Personalization → Memory → Memory summary**.

## Filled example (Sam's difficult client)

```
Nadia is my main contact at the children's book publisher, and my biggest
client. She hates surprises and works months ahead. If anything about a
deadline or the scope might change, she needs a calm heads-up early, never
a last-minute bomb. She responds well to short, warm, confident messages
and badly to over-apologizing. Right now we are mid-way through a 20-card
set due at the end of the month.
```

## Privacy line

Put in what helps and what you are comfortable having stored. Keep out other
people's real secrets, medical details, and anything you would not want to
resurface. (Full privacy rules: Chapter 23.)
