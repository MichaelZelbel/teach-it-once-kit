# About-You Template (Chapter 5)

Copy the block below, fill it in with your own life, and paste it into
ChatGPT under **Settings → Personalization**, in the **About you** section,
box **"More about you"** ("Interests, values, or preferences to keep in
mind"). Fill **Nickname** and **Occupation** while you are there, then save.

Keep it to a few tight paragraphs. Dense and specific beats long and vague. Delete any line that does not change how a
smart assistant would actually help you today.

```
I'm [name], a [what you do / your situation]. [One line on how your days
usually go, and anything that shapes your time.]

People who matter: [the handful your assistant should know, by role: team,
clients, family. Note who to loop in on what.]

Focused on right now: [your live priorities and the projects actually on
your plate this month, with any deadlines.]

How to help me: [answer first then why? short bullets or full prose? blunt
or gentle? Ask before assuming things?]

Never: [the hard limits. Tone you hate, things it should never suggest,
words it should never use.]
```

## Filled example (a made-up freelance illustrator)

```
I'm Sam, a freelance illustrator working from home. I juggle 3 regular
clients plus one-off jobs. Mornings until 1pm are my drawing hours and I
protect them hard; calls and admin go in the afternoon.

People who matter: my agent Dana handles contracts (loop her in on money
questions); my three clients are a board-game studio, a children's book
publisher, and a coffee brand; my partner Alex shares my calendar.

Focused on right now: finishing a 20-card illustration set (due end of
month), raising my rates for new clients, and building a portfolio site.

How to help me: answer first, then the short why. I like tight bullet
lists. Ask before assuming a deadline. If I'm overthinking, tell me.

Never: write in stiff corporate language, suggest I work through the
night, or put exclamation marks in my client emails. I sound calm and
warm, never salesy.
```
